Mount Sparrowhawk
29-06-2025

2025 Peaks

Before heading off, I chose to leave my boots and crampons in the car and just take my axe. This ended up being a bad decision that I would regret later!

Roughly 1.2km in, I lost the trail and ended up going north following a different trail. I just decided to head directly south off the trail and eventually ran into the correct trail again.

300m from the summit, I made the tough decision not to go any further. I was exhausted and not properly equipped for the terrain. The snow so far had been up to my thigh, and I was only wearing low-top hiking shoes. If I would attempt this mountain again, I would go in August instead and bring boots and crampons to change into if needed.
